About Healthy Day

Healthy Day is a health-focused mobile design that encourages users to track their meals, workouts, and daily mood. The app was designed with simplicity and clarity in mind to create a calm and organized user experience. The project highlights user research, accessibility, and aesthetic balance.

  • Type: UI/UX Mobile App Design
  • Tools: Figma, FigJam, Prototyping, User Testing
  • Year: 2025
  • Focus: Wellness, Habit Tracking, Minimalist Design

Problems & Solutions

Challenges faced during the design process and how they were addressed to enhance user experience.

1. Defining User Needs

Problem: It was difficult to determine what health features were most valuable to users.

Solution: Conducted surveys and interviews to identify key user goals such as tracking meals, mood, and exercise consistency.

2. Balancing Simplicity and Functionality

Problem: Adding too many options made the interface look cluttered.

Solution: Simplified navigation and grouped related actions using clean icons and soft color palettes.

3. Consistent Visual Language

Problem: Maintaining consistency across multiple screens was challenging during iteration.

Solution: Used Figma components and a shared style guide for typography and colors to ensure visual harmony.
